# Gridee — Website Overview

*Reviewed: 27 July 2026 | Primary source: [gridee.in](https://www.gridee.in/)*

## What Gridee does

Gridee is an India-focused, AI-driven parking ecosystem. It helps drivers find, reserve, enter, pay for, and track parking digitally, while giving parking operators the tools and optional on-ground infrastructure to run sites efficiently. Its aim is to remove the usual parking friction: searching for a space, queues at gates, paper tickets, and disconnected payment or access processes.

Gridee can be deployed as software alone or as a complete operating stack that combines its apps, cloud platform, ANPR (automatic number-plate recognition), QR access, boom-barrier integration, analytics, and trained site operators.

## What it offers

### For drivers and users

- **Live parking discovery:** View nearby/available spaces, location details, prices, distance, and—where provided—amenities and map navigation.
- **Reservation and booking management:** Reserve a space, see active and past bookings, and use the booking/check-in QR. The Android listing also describes extending a booking while on the move.
- **Contactless entry and exit:** Use a secure QR code or vehicle number-plate validation for check-in and check-out, reducing ticketing and gate delays.
- **Wallet and cashless payments:** Pay through a digital wallet and supported payment methods; view balances, transactions, parking activity, and history. The website names UPI, cards, and wallets as payment options.
- **Notifications and account tools:** Receive booking and entry/exit updates; manage vehicles, settings, support, and communication/privacy preferences.

### For parking operators and organizations

- **Cloud parking-management dashboard:** Manage parking lots, users, operators, availability/occupancy, bookings, payments, support tickets, and live reporting from one system.
- **Operational controls:** Operators can manage vehicle access and occupancy. Gridee also offers trained on-ground operators for scanning, exceptions, entry/exit flow, and daily coordination.
- **Analytics:** Live occupancy, usage, and operational insights intended to support allocation and site decisions.
- **ANPR service:** Camera-led number-plate recognition validates vehicles against live bookings and access rules, automating entry/exit and improving access control.
- **Boom-barrier integration:** Optional gate automation for sites that require controlled vehicle access.
- **Enterprise deployment:** Positioned for universities/institutions, apartments, corporate offices, hospitals, shopping malls, metro stations/transit networks, public lots, and smart-city deployments.

## How a typical parking session works

1. A user signs in, discovers a parking location, sees availability, and reserves a space.
2. The app keeps the booking, wallet/payment, and parking information together.
3. At the site, the vehicle is validated by QR or number plate; ANPR and/or an operator can handle access.
4. A connected barrier can open automatically where installed, while the platform records the session and keeps time, access, and payment in sync.

## Technology and platform components

Gridee describes its stack as a connected system of a consumer mobile app, cloud platform, management software, AI engine, ANPR cameras, real-time analytics, and smart infrastructure. Its published materials also reference QR scanning, optical character recognition, cloud APIs, and secure payment integration. The mobile experience is available on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gridee.parking) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/grideeapp/id6757460398).

## Security, privacy, and data controls

- Gridee states that it does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
- It uses data to operate bookings, verify access, process payments, and support users. Its policy lists account details, registered vehicle number, parking activity, wallet/transaction data, technical/device data, and QR/number-plate images used for recognition.
- The site says data in transit is protected with TLS and access is limited to authorized systems and personnel on a need-to-know basis.
- Payments are processed through Razorpay; Gridee says it does not store card or banking details.
- Users can request account deletion by emailing `gridee.business@gmail.com` from their registered address. The site says deletion is irreversible and removes account history, balances, and preferences. Users may also request booking and transaction history.

## Current positioning and roadmap notes

Gridee presents itself as “AI-driven parking infrastructure for India’s future cities,” with a particular focus on smoother commuter arrivals and scalable urban parking operations. Its Google Play listing additionally describes filters, interactive maps, turn-by-turn navigation, price comparison, and transparent pricing. That listing marks reviews/ratings, monthly subscriptions, corporate parking solutions, calendar-integrated bookings, and a loyalty programme as **coming soon**; these should not be treated as confirmed current website features.
